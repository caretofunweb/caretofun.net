## 🌟 Interactive Reader Features
- **Page Flip Animation:** Experience authentic 3D page curl effects as you progress through each chapter.
- **Page Jump Input:** Type any page number in the bottom toolbar to skip directly to your favorite scene.
- **Sound Effects:** Enjoy a subtle page-turn sound (which can be muted anytime via the 🔊 Sound button).
- **Fullscreen Mode:** Read without distractions on desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone.
- **Mobile Friendly:** Automatically adapts to single-page portrait reading on smartphones and dual-page book reading on wide computer monitors.

## ✍️ How to Publish Your Own PDF Novels
If you are an author or content creator publishing on our website, you can now embed any PDF book into your blog posts with a single click:
1. Open your **Admin Panel** at `/admin/`.
2. Click **New Blog Post**.
3. In the toolbar, click the new **`📖 PDF Novel Flipbook`** button.
4. Enter the URL of your uploaded PDF file and the novel title.
5. Hit **Publish**!
